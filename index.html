“ The dual imperative

The modern workforce executive holds two jobs at once: rigorous public stewardship and visionary systems leadership.

One demands accountability, compliance, and the careful handling of public trust. The other demands vision — the ability to align partners you don't control and move whole regions forward. This fellowship is built for leaders who refuse to choose between them.

What you'll be equipped to do

Five capabilities that define an executive.

You'll leave with more than ideas. You'll leave with the judgment, relationships, and playbooks to lead at the top of your organization.

Master board governance

Build a powerful, trusting partnership with your Board Chair — and learn to govern with clarity, not just comply.

Lead with fiscal & performance excellence

Turn compliance into a strategic tool. Use fiscal discipline and performance data to steer, not just to survive an audit.

Align complex systems

Lead partners you don't directly manage. Move coalitions of agencies, colleges, and employers toward shared goals.

Drive regional strategy

Align workforce development with economic development to shape local and regional strategies that actually take hold.

Gain an elite peer network

Join a trusted circle of fellow executives for candid support, shared best practices, and a phone list you'll use for years.

The experience

A year in four sessions.

Four in-person gatherings across California anchor the year. Between them, the real work happens — in your own organization, with your cohort.

1
January · Sacramento

Foundation

A full-day foundation session at the Foundation for California Community Colleges to set the frameworks for the year.

2
April · Irvine

Deep Practice

Two half-days following WORKCON — applying governance and fiscal frameworks to real cases.

3
July

Working Session

A full-day working session to pressure-test capstone projects and sharpen your systems-leadership approach.

4
September · Monterey

Capstone

Capstone Presentations, connected to the Meeting of the Minds conference — your year, delivered to your peers.

Between sessions, fellows apply the frameworks inside their own organizations and stay connected with the cohort — turning ideas into practice, and colleagues into a network.

Schedule shown reflects the 2026 cohort and is illustrative of the annual rhythm. Specific dates and venues are confirmed each year.

Investment & expectations

A serious commitment — by design.

$2,950 Program fee · CWA members only
  • Discounted registration for the Meeting of the Minds conference.
  • Standing invitations to CWA's Quarterly Board Meetings.
  • A full year of faculty access and cohort membership.

The program fee does not include travel, lodging, or parking for in-person sessions.

What's expected of fellows

  • Show up. Attendance at all four in-person sessions across the year.
  • Stay engaged. Active participation with the cohort between sessions.
  • Deliver. A capstone presentation that puts the year to work in your organization.

This is a commitment — and that's the point. The fellowship is only as strong as the leaders who fully show up for it.
