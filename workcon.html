About Workcon

Where the work gets done.

Workcon is CWA's practitioner conference — three days built for the people delivering workforce services on the ground. Where Meeting of the Minds convenes executive leaders, Workcon is for the frontline: case managers, business-services teams, program staff, and the specialists who make California's America's Job Centers run.

Expect practical, skills-focused sessions, peer problem-solving, and the connections that make the daily work easier. Join your colleagues from across the state in San Diego this April.
