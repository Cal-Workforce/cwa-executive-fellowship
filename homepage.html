Who we are

Driving workforce innovation across California.

We're the connective tissue of California's workforce system — carrying the field's voice outward and building its capacity inward, so 45 local boards can do their best work.

Amplify

We carry the field's voice.

We bring our members' stories and needs to the people who shape policy and funding — so California's boards speak with one credible, coordinated voice, and decision-makers act on it.

Equip

We give boards what they need to act.

We translate policy into plain-language action, move resources to where they're needed most, and build the capacity boards need to meet whatever comes next.

Connect

We bring the system together.

We convene boards and partners — traditional and nontraditional alike — to learn from one another, solve shared problems, and shape what comes next. No board works alone.
