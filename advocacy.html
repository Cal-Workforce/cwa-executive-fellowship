CWA-Sponsored Bill Officially in Print · Feb 19, 2026

AB 2300 (Arambula): Strengthening Workforce Development

Assembly Bill 2300 modernizes the outdated administrative requirements that slow workforce investments, and authorizes the Employment Development Department (EDD) to issue operational directives in consultation with local boards — so support reaches communities faster.

Streamlined funding

Modernizes the subgrant mechanisms for federal and state workforce funds.

System responsiveness

Improves the speed of delivery to local workforce development boards.

Stakeholder collaboration

Ensures EDD guidance is developed with local board input.
