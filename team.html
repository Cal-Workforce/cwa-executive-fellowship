About Us

Our team.

A small team carrying a big charge: championing California's 45 local workforce boards, and the communities they serve.

Leadership & staff

Adam Peck

Executive Director

Adam leads CWA's statewide advocacy, policy, and capacity-building work for California's 45 local workforce boards. He spent 28 years in the field — 18 as Executive Director of the Workforce Investment Board of Tulare County — and brings a data-driven, equity-first approach to connecting people with meaningful work.

Kelli Oropeza-Grimes

Chief Financial Officer

Kelli keeps CWA financially strong and operationally sharp. A seasoned finance executive, she blends deep expertise in accounting and value-based management with a gift for turning complex numbers into clear decisions — so the organization can stay focused on serving the field.

Tom Price

Chief Impact Officer

Tom leads CWA's push to design services around the people who actually use them. A former community journalist turned human-centered designer, he founded the design firm Middlestate and served as a Workforce Transformation Corps Fellow. He believes people, given a real seat at the table, make systems better.

Isis Orellana

Policy Analyst

Isis tracks and translates the policy and budget decisions that shape California's workforce boards. She came to CWA from the California State Assembly, where she served as a Senior District Representative — experience that sharpened her read on how Sacramento works and what it means for the field.
